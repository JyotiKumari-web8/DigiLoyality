Official Documentation & Operational Manual

DigiLoyalty Pro

Complete 3-Phase Operational Guide & Running Manual

Platform: Digital Loyalty & Reseller Engine • Database: Turnkey SQLite • Server: PHP 8.3 Native

1. The 3-Phase Lifecycle Overview

DigiLoyalty Pro is structured into three clean, interconnected operational phases designed to onboard local retail businesses and turn first-time visitors into loyal repeat customers.

Phase 1: Master Admin

You (The Agency Reseller)

Owns the platform, onboards local business owners, manages client subscription plans, and collects SaaS revenue.

Phase 2: Business Owner

Store Merchant (Your Client)

Logs into their portal, builds their loyalty card, enrolls customers, tracks Repeat Visit Rate, and operates the billing terminal.

Phase 3: Customer

The Shopper / Diner

Logs in with mobile number to an account already created by the store. Collects stamps, earns coins, and redeems rewards.

💡 The Core Loyalty Philosophy: In retail and dining, a customer who visits once is just an acquisition. Loyalty begins the exact moment they return for their 1st repeat visit (Visit #2). The system automatically marks customers who visit > 1 time as Repeat Customers and tracks this as the primary business health KPI.

2. Quick Start & Server Launch

The project is fully self-contained with SQLite and requires zero external database setup, zero passwords, and zero configuration.

1
Double-Click the Launcher: Open your project folder and double-click run.bat.
2
Manual Terminal Command: Alternatively, in PowerShell run:
php -d extension=pdo_sqlite -S 127.0.0.1:8000 -t public
3
Open Browser: Visit http://localhost:8000. The application is immediately live.

3. Phase 1: Master Admin (You as the Reseller)

As the Master Admin, you operate the white-label agency control center to onboard merchants and manage subscriptions.

A. Master Admin Setup & Login

  • URL: http://localhost:8000/admin/login
  • Initial Setup: If launching for the first time, the portal automatically presents the "Create Master Admin" setup screen. Enter your Agency Name, Your Name, Admin Email, and Admin Password.
  • Login: On subsequent visits, enter your registered email and password to access the Reseller Console.

B. Onboarding a Client Business (Selling Your Service)

When you close a sale with a local cafe, salon, or store, you onboard them directly from your console:

1
Click the blue "+ Onboard New Merchant" button in the top navigation.
2
Fill Store Details:
  • Business Name: e.g. Royal Brew Cafe
  • Category: Restaurant & Cafe, Salon & Spa, Retail Boutique, Gym, etc.
  • Owner Name & Phone: The business owner's contact info.
  • Owner Login Email & Password: The credentials the store owner will use to access their dashboard.
  • Subscription Plan: Choose 30 Days (Trial), 90 Days, Annual (365 Days), or Lifetime VIP.
  • Stamp Target & Reward: e.g. 8 Stamps = Free Gourmet Coffee / ₹150 Off.
3
Click "Create & Deliver Credentials". The merchant is created instantly.

4. Phase 2: Business Owner (Store Merchant)

The business owner logs in to configure their digital loyalty card, enroll customers, and manage checkouts.

A. Owner Login & Dashboard

  • URL: http://localhost:8000/login
  • Credentials: Owner Email and Password created by Admin in Phase 1.
  • Dashboard KPIs: Displays Repeat Visit Rate (%), Total Customers, Visits This Week, and Coins Issued.

B. Visual Card Builder & Standee

  • Card Settings: /owner/card-settings — Customize primary/secondary brand colors, stamp count (e.g. 6, 8, 10 stamps), reward title, and coin earning rate.
  • Print Counter Standee: /owner/standee — Generates a print-ready table tent card with the store's unique QR code to display on billing counters.

5. How Business Owners Add Customers (1st Visit)

When a customer visits the store for the first time, the owner or cashier enrolls them using either of two methods:

Method A: Cashier Speed Terminal (During Checkout — 3 Seconds)

1
Open the Cashier Terminal at http://localhost:8000/staff/terminal.
2
Type the customer's 10-digit mobile number on the keypad and click "Search Customer".
3
If new, enter their name (e.g. "Ananya"), input the bill amount (optional), and click "+1 Visit Stamp & Credit Coins".
4
Result: Account is created, Visit #1 is stamped, and welcome coins are awarded with confetti animation.

Method B: Owner Dashboard (Customer CRM)

1
Go to /owner/customers and click "+ Enroll New Customer".
2
Enter Customer Mobile (10 digits), Full Name, Email (optional), and Welcome Reward Coins (default 20).
3
Click "Enroll & Create Card". The customer profile is immediately active.

6. Phase 3: Customer Login & Loyalty Pass

The customer logs into the account already created for them by the owner with zero app downloads.

1
Access Link: Customer scans the store counter QR standee or opens http://localhost:8000/card/{slug}.
2
1-Step Sign In: Enters their 10-digit mobile number.
3
Live Digital Pass Display: The system recognizes their account created by the owner and displays:
  • Apple Wallet Card: Branded header with store logo, customer name, and tier status.
  • Stamp Punch Circles: Dashed circles showing filled stars for stamps earned and a gold gift icon for the final goal.
  • Reward Coins Wallet: Live coin balance with real Rupee value equivalent.
  • Personal QR Code: Dynamic QR code for the cashier to scan during counter checkout.
  • WhatsApp Share Link: 1-click button to share referral pass with friends.

7. The Reward Redemption Process

The system provides two redemption mechanisms to reward customer loyalty:

Type 1: Milestone Stamp Reward (e.g. Free Item after 8 Stamps)

Workflow:
  1. Customer visits store and completes their required stamps (e.g. 8th stamp).
  2. A unique alphanumeric voucher code (e.g. RW-8D4F2A) is automatically generated in their pass under "Claimed Vouchers".
  3. On their next visit, the customer shows the voucher code to the cashier.
  4. The cashier looks up the customer in the terminal and clicks "Redeem Voucher".
  5. The system marks the voucher as Redeemed with a timestamp, preventing reuse.

Type 2: Reward Coin Bill Discount (Spend Cashback)

Workflow:
  1. Customer accumulates coins on every purchase (e.g. 1 coin per ₹10 spent).
  2. At checkout, customer requests to use their coins: "Redeem 50 coins for a discount."
  3. In the Cashier Terminal under "Redeem Coins for Bill Discount", cashier enters 50 and clicks "Apply Discount".
  4. 50 coins are debited from the customer's wallet, and the discount equivalent (e.g. ₹25.00) is deducted from the final bill.
